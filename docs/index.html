It speaks up New

Daybook notices things, so you don't have to.

Turn on Suggestions and Daybook gently flags what's slipping. You choose how: in the app only, in your morning briefing, or as a notification. Quiet hours and a limit of two a day keep it from nagging.

Slipped through?

Your reminder fires once. If a task is still open two days later, Daybook brings it back up, once, and lets it go when you reschedule or finish it.

Spending, as it happens

An iPhone Shortcut tells Daybook each time you tap Apple Pay. See today's total on Today, get asked about big purchases, and have bar tabs counted against a weekly goal if you set one.

Only the amount, card and kind of place are sent. Never the store's name or where you are.
What it does

One place for the small stuff that runs your life.

🔔

Reminders that buzz your phone

One-off or repeating ("weekdays at 8am"). Tap Done right on the notification and it's logged. No extra app to install.

📝

Log anything in seconds

Meals, drinks, meds, workouts, sleep, moods, or your own log types. Track time spent and see it add up.

☀️

Today, at a glance

Your top priorities, what's overdue and a timeline of your day. Opt in to a morning and evening briefing on your phone.

📖

Journal and weekly review

Each day's activity and reflections in one place. Ask your assistant for a weekly review: what moved, what slipped, what to focus on next.

🎯

Goals that move

Write down why it matters and how you'll measure it, then add next steps with dates and log progress as you go.

🧠

Memory that's yours

Notes, projects, decisions and reference, searchable by you and your assistant. Download everything as Markdown any time.

📅

Works with your calendar

Your dated tasks show up in Apple, Google or Outlook calendar, and your calendar's events can show up in Daybook.

✅

Apple Reminders sync

Two-way sync through an iPhone Shortcut, set up from Settings. Reminders handles the alerts, so you never get two, and you can check things off from your Watch, Siri or lock screen.

💳

Spending at a glance

Apple Pay taps add up on a Spending page: today, this week, and each tap. Optional daily or weekly targets, and your evening briefing says what you spent.

🛒

Errands where you are

Tag a task @at:grocery, and when you park near a grocery store your phone shows the list. Your location never leaves your phone.

💬

Suggestions, your way

Nudges about postponed and overdue tasks, in the app, in your morning briefing, or on your phone. Each one says why, and you can snooze, dismiss or mute it.

📱

An app on your home screen

Add it to your iPhone or Android home screen. When there's an update, tap Update now and it updates itself.

Privacy, without mystery

Your Daybook runs in your account, not ours.

Each person runs their own copy in their own free Cloudflare account. Notes are kept in that app, or as Markdown files in your own private GitHub repository if you prefer. Nobody else has access, including whoever sent you the link.

Plainly: this is not end-to-end encrypted. Your own app, and assistants you connect, can read your notes so they can help you.

How it works ↗
  • Free to runFits in Cloudflare's free plan. No subscription, no card.
  • Leave any timeDownload every note as plain Markdown files.
  • Open sourceRead every line of code on GitHub.
  • Updates when you say soNothing changes until you tap Update now.
  • Extras are off until you turn them onSuggestions, spending and errand reminders each have their own switch. Spending keeps amounts and kinds of place, never store names, and can be erased any time.
