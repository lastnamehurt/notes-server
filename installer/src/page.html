Install Daybook

Your own private notes app, running in your own free Cloudflare account. About 5 minutes, and no coding.

1Make a free Cloudflare account

Cloudflare runs your app. It's free for personal use and doesn't ask for a card.

  1. Sign up, then open the email Cloudflare sends you and tap the link to verify your address.
  2. Come back here. (Already have an account? Skip to step 2.)
Sign up at Cloudflare

2Give Daybook a key to your account

This lets the installer set up your app, and later lets your app update itself. The button fills in exactly what's needed.

  1. Tap Create my key and sign in to Cloudflare if asked.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Continue to summary, then Create Token.
  3. Tap Copy next to the key, come back here and paste it below.
Create my key

Is this safe?

The key only works on your Cloudflare account, and only for apps and their storage. This page passes it straight to Cloudflare and doesn't keep it. Your app keeps a copy, in your own account, so it can install updates when you tap Update now. You can delete the key any time in Cloudflare (My Profile → API Tokens); your app keeps working, it just can't update itself.

Daybook is open source: read the code.

3Choose your address and password

Your app will be at daybook.yourname.workers.dev. Lowercase letters, numbers and dashes; pick something you like, since it's part of every app address in your Cloudflare account.

You'll use it to open your app and to connect Claude. Save it somewhere safe, like your phone's password manager.

4You're done
